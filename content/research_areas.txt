Human and social behavior: routines, habits, mobility.
The C2S2 investigates how individuals navigate their daily lives by analyzing patterns in individual routines, habits, and mobility. Using large-scale digital traces – from mobile phone data, IoT and IoB sensors, and social platforms – the Center explores how people move through social, physical, and online spaces, adapt to changing environments, and develop habitual behaviors. These insights help reveal how individual decisions aggregate into broader social patterns, with implications for social dynamics, political, economic, and productive systems, urban planning, transportation systems, and behavioral interventions.

Collective behavior and action: democracy, decision making, cultural and social dynamics.
Understanding how groups form opinions, make decisions, and engage in democratic processes is a central focus of the C2S2. The Center studies how collective behavior and action emerges from interactions among individuals, particularly in contexts such as political participation, cultural evolution, and online communities. By leveraging generative AI and deep learning models, as well as complex systems, network analysis, agent-based modeling, and behavioral data, the Center examines the mechanisms that drive consensus, polarization, cooperation, and the spread of information in society, with a particular focus on the increasing role of generative AI in shaping these processes.

Demography, social inequalities, labour market, and welfare systems dynamics
The C2S2 addresses key societal challenges by analyzing demographic changetrends, economic disparities, and the evolving nature of work and welfare. Using quantitative and computational methods, the Center investigates how social inequalities are produced and reproduced across different populations, and how labor markets and welfare systems respond to technological, demographic, and policy changes. Special attention is paid to demographic behavioursissues, including household formation, fertility, and population ageing. This research informs evidence-based strategies for promoting social equity and inclusive development.

Computational epidemiology and public health
At the intersection of data science and public health, the C2S2 conducts research on the spread of diseases, health behaviors, and population-level interventions. Through computational modeling, real-time data analysis, and simulations, the Center studies how health dynamics unfold in society, especially in response to pandemics, vaccination campaigns, and behavioral risk factors. Furthermore, the C2S2 investigates the potential role of AI in addressing pandemic preparedness, infectious disease modeling, and public health interventions, as well as in simulating population dynamics and human behaviors. This work supports public health decision-making and aims to enhance societal resilience through proactive and data-driven strategies.
