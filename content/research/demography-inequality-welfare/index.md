The C2S2 addresses key societal challenges by analyzing demographic trends, economic disparities, and the evolving nature of work and welfare. Using quantitative and computational methods, the Center investigates how social inequalities are produced and reproduced across different populations, and how labor markets and welfare systems respond to technological, demographic, and policy changes. Special attention is paid to demographic behaviour, including household formation, fertility, and population ageing. This research informs evidence-based strategies for promoting social equity and inclusive development.
