At the intersection of data science and public health, the C2S2 conducts research on the spread of diseases, health behaviors, and population-level interventions. Through computational modeling, real-time data analysis, and simulations, the Center studies how health dynamics unfold in society, especially in response to pandemics, vaccination campaigns, and behavioral risk factors. Furthermore, the C2S2 investigates the potential role of AI in addressing pandemic preparedness, infectious disease modeling, and public health interventions, as well as in simulating population dynamics and human behaviors. This work supports public health decision-making and aims to enhance societal resilience through proactive and data-driven strategies.
