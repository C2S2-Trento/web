The C2S2 investigates how individuals navigate their daily lives by analyzing patterns in individual routines, habits, and mobility. Using large-scale digital traces—from mobile phone data, IoT and IoB sensors, and social platforms—the Center explores how people move through social, physical, and online spaces, adapt to changing environments, and develop habitual behaviors. These insights help reveal how individual decisions aggregate into broader social patterns, with implications for social dynamics, political, economic, and productive systems, urban planning, transportation systems, and behavioral interventions.
