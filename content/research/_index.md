C2S2 investigates everyday human behaviour, collective action, demographic and welfare dynamics, and public health. Across these four areas, we combine computational models, quantitative methods and digital data to address social questions.

Our overarching theme is artificial intelligence and society: how AI can help us understand human societies, how it transforms social life, and how societies of artificial agents behave and evolve.
