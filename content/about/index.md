C2S2 was established in September 2021 by the University of Trento’s Department of Sociology and Social Research, its Department of Economics and Management, and Fondazione Bruno Kessler.

We connect social science, computational modelling and data science to understand social and economic systems. Our work links basic research, applied research and teaching.

## Our mission

We support interdisciplinary collaboration, scientific exchange and training. We develop methods for understanding human behaviour and the social changes associated with new technologies, and share this knowledge with institutions and the public.
